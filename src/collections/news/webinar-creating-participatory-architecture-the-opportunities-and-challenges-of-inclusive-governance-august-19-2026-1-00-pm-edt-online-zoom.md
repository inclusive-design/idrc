The Partnerships for Financial Inclusion invites you to a webinar with Madeline Toubiana (University of Ottawa) and Natalie Eng (University of Alberta). The speakers will explore the possibilities of Participatory Architecture as a community-led and inclusive governance structure, discussing the benefits and challenges of this form of governance and the importance of alignment with key organizational objectives.

This webinar is part of Financial Inclusion & Resilience Movement (FIRM)'s governance co-design process, facilitated by the Inclusive Design Research Centre at OCAD University. The event is free, accessible and open to the public. Accessibility needs can be indicated in the registration form.
